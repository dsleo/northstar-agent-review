08

The Sierra recommendation

Plan & Upgrade Advisor Offers a Clearer Path

Concrete, measurable outcome

The customer completes an eligible plan or device upgrade: a transaction we can verify immediately, compare against today's completion rate, and translate into incremental contribution margin.

Smaller pool, chosen on purpose

The only one the agent can resolve start to finish.

  • Billing mostly needs a correction a human still has to make.
  • Technical mostly needs network intervention outside the agent's control.

Expected monthly completions

236k uncontained × 50% eligible × 60% complete without handoff  =  ~71k upgrades / month
